About the project

What it is

The One Piece Character Generator rolls a new character from the world of One Piece and follows their journey across the Grand Line. You can type a name or let the generator invent one, and it builds their race, affiliation and rank, fighting style, Haki, Devil Fruit, feats, Doriki and Bounty. Then it shows how all of that changes one year in, two years later and ten years after.

Why we built it

We are One Piece fans, and the series is full of people whose strength, reputation and place in the world change over time. We wanted a way to imagine our own pirate, marine, revolutionary or hybrid, instead of only reading about the existing cast. The goal is a tool that tells that kind of story from a blank sheet and is quick enough to play with between chapters.

How it works

Every probability and value comes from plain CSV files in the project's data folder, so the rules are easy to read and to change. The generator runs entirely in your browser: there are no accounts, no server keeps your characters, and no images are generated with AI. Portraits you upload stay on your device.

Source and credits
